description: Relationship tone and original-media constraints for this birthday scrapbook.
---

This birthday website is a deeply caring gift for a beloved sister/friend, not a romantic gift. Avoid cheesy romantic framing.

**Why:** The user explicitly established the relationship tone.

**How to apply:** Keep future copy, captions, and visual additions affectionate without implying romance.

Use the supplied personal photos and videos as the core media. Preserve the subject's appearance naturally; do not substitute stock photos or external copyrighted media.

**Why:** The user wants a personal digital scrapbook built from real memories.

**How to apply:** Retain original photos and video content when making future media or design changes.
